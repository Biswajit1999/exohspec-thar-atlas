# Research maturity audit: v0.2 → v0.3

The evidence rubric rises from **52/100** to **93/100**. Scores are an internal,
traceable completeness rubric, not peer review and not a literal claim of an
order-of-magnitude increase in scientific truth.

The largest gains are ground-truth recovery (0→10), uncertainty calibration
(3→8), deterministic reproducibility (4→9), and automated testing (3→9). The
measured-data boundary remains unchanged: no raw private FITS data, atomic
identification, or wavelength solution is published.

See [`RESEARCH_MATURITY_AUDIT.csv`](RESEARCH_MATURITY_AUDIT.csv) for the
dimension-level scores and repository paths cited by each evidence statement.
